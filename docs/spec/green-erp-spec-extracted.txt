English
===== PAGE 2 =====
The project aims to develop a centralized and integrated platform for managing end-
to-end e-commerce operations. The system will cover the complete order lifecycle,
starting from connecting online stores and sales channels, receiving and confirming
orders, managing inventory and fulfillment, shipping and delivery follow-up, and
ending with collections, expenses, financial reconciliation, and operational reporting.
The platform will be designed to serve businesses operating one or multiple online
stores, as well as companies providing order management, warehousing, fulfillment,
and shipping services to third-party merchants under a Fulfillment Center or Third-
Party Logistics model.
The system must support integrations with e-commerce platforms such as Shopify
and WooCommerce, in addition to marketplaces such as Amazon and Noon. The
architecture should also allow additional platforms to be connected in the future
through APIs.
The platform must support manual order creation, file-based order imports, and
automated order synchronization from connected stores and marketplaces.

The system must support multiple operating models, allowing each business to select
the model that matches its operational structure.

for each store.

party merchants.

English
The platform must use a multi-level account structure.
The proposed hierarchy is:
System
Contains all companies registered on the platform.
Company
===== PAGE 23 =====
Represents the organization using the system or providing fulfillment services.
Merchant
Represents the owner of the products or brand.
Store
Represents a merchant sales channel, such as a Shopify store, Amazon seller
account, or Noon store.

stores, and the merchant’s inventory may be distributed across multiple warehouses.

Customization
